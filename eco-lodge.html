Johnsteps Eco-Lodge – Kinigi, Rwanda

Johnsteps Eco-Lodge is a peaceful, nature-friendly lodge located in Kinigi, Musanze District, near Volcanoes National Park. The lodge is designed for travelers who love nature, culture, and responsible tourism. Our eco-lodge combines comfort, sustainability, and local experience, offering guests a relaxing stay while supporting environmental conservation and the local community.

Johnsteps Eco-Lodge, Rwanda

Johnsteps Eco-Lodge is located in Kinigi, just a short drive from: Volcanoes National Park Headquarters (10 minutes walk), Gorilla trekking starting points (30 minutes walk), Golden monkey tracking areas (30 minutes walk), Virunga volcano views (Simply standing within the Johnsteps Eco-Lodge compound, you are rewarded with uninterrupted views of the majestic Gorilla Mountains). The location makes it ideal for gorilla trekkers, nature lovers, researchers, and adventure travelers.
