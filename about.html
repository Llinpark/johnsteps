Our Story

Where Passion Meets Purpose

Driven by an obsession with the untamed, Johnsteps Safaris was born to shatter the "ordinary" holiday. We specialize in the deep dive—curating high-octane adventures and intimate cultural encounters across Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda, and Tanzania for those who demand more from their miles.

We don’t just visit communities; we belong to them. Every Johnsteps expedition is a pact of respect—a bridge to the hidden Africa. By partnering with indigenous guardians and cultural ambassadors, we unlock secret landscapes and ancient traditions, ensuring your journey leaves a footprint of growth and a legacy of preservation.

Milestones in Our Adventure

Key moments that shaped who we are today

2015

The Spark of Discovery

What began as John Mike's personal quest for the untamed turned into a movement. Driven by a relentless curiosity, he swapped the beaten path for the hidden trail, founding Johnsteps Safaris to share the continent’s best-kept secrets with a new generation of explorers.

2017

Beyond Borders: The Four-Country Frontier

Mastery knows no boundaries. We scaled our vision by establishing deep-rooted partnerships with the legendary guardians of East Africa’s most iconic landscapes. From the Serengeti to the Volcanoes National Park, we brought the "Johnsteps Way" to the region's four most breathtaking nations.

2021

The Kinigi Impact: Investing in People

At the foot of the volcanoes, we found our heart. By founding the Imigano Eco-cooperative, Johnsteps Safaris moved beyond "sightseeing" to deep-rooted community support. Today, we are proud to stand with over 200 families, proving that every journey you take with us directly strengthens the hands that hold the heritage of Rwanda.

2025

A Global Seal, A Local Promise

In a world of "greenwashing," we chose the path of transparency. Achieving B-Corp certification solidified our status as a global leader in ethical travel. Our 2030 roadmap is clear: to move beyond "no-trace" tourism and become the first regenerative force in East African adventure—balancing profit with a profound purpose for people and the planet.
