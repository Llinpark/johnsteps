Kenya Tour Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the heart of East Africa, where iconic wildlife roams across vast golden savannahs. This comprehensive safari takes you from the legendary Maasai Mara plains to the scenic shores of Diani Beach.

Experience the Great Migration, track the elusive Big Five, and immerse yourself in the rich traditions of the Maasai people. Witness breathtaking sunsets over Mount Kilimanjaro while enjoying the finest hospitality and luxury throughout your entire Kenyan adventure.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Nairobi

Welcome to Kenya’s capital. Check into your city hotel and enjoy a welcome dinner featuring delicious and authentic local specialties.

Welcome Dinner
Day 2

Nairobi City Tour

Visit the Giraffe Centre, feed orphaned elephants at Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, and enjoy a traditional lunch at the famous Carnivore.

Breakfast, Lunch
Day 3

Amboseli National Park

Travel to Amboseli for views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Enjoy an afternoon game drive to see massive elephant herds and predators.

Full Board
Days 4-5

Great Rift Valley

Explore Lake Nakuru’s flamingos and rhinos. Continue to Lake Naivasha for a boat safari and walking tour at Crescent Island.

Full Board
Days 6-8

Maasai Mara Magic

Spend three days exploring the legendary Mara. Witness the Great Migration, track the Big Five, and visit a Maasai village.

Full Board
Days 9-11

Diani Beach Bliss

Fly to the coast for three relaxing days. Enjoy white sands, turquoise waters, snorkeling, and fresh seafood in this tropical paradise.

All Inclusive
Day 12

Departure

Transfer to the airport for your flight home. Take memories of an incredible Kenyan adventure that will last a lifetime.

Breakfast
