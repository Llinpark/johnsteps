Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda

A Verdant Wilderness

Discover the Magic

Perched atop the mist-covered peaks of the Virunga Mountains, Volcanoes National Park captivates visitors with its iconic mountain gorillas, lush bamboo forests, and rich biodiversity. This enchanting Rwandan wilderness offers a perfect blend of thrill and serenity, from trekking through ancient rainforests to encountering endangered golden monkeys in dramatic volcanic settings.

Must-Visit Attractions

Experience the best of what Rwanda has to offer

Akagera National Park

Famous for its breathtaking savannah sunsets and diverse wildlife, home to the iconic Big Five and sweeping wetland landscapes.

Nyungwe Forest National Park

A unique mountain forest with dramatic cascading waterfalls and hidden valley views perfect for hiking and primate encounters.

Lake Kivu (Rubavu & Karongi)

Explore the hidden coves and tranquil islands of a massive Rift Valley lake perfect for kayaking and sunset boat cruises.

Kigali Genocide Memorial & City Tour

The vibrant capital offering a moving journey through the Genocide Memorial, local artisan markets, and spectacular views of the rolling green hills.

The King’s Palace Museum (Nyanza)

Witness exceptional ritual performances while enjoying panoramic views of the meticulously restored royal residence and rolling Nyanza hills.

Iby'iwacu Cultural Village

Take a seat at a traditional wedding ceremony and discover the medicinal secrets of the forest within this living heritage museum.

Essential Travel Information

Everything you need to know for your "Land of a Thousand Hills" adventure.

The Primate Trekking View

June to September for the best trekking conditions. Rainy seasons offer the best chimpanzee sightings.

Weather & Climate

Tropical highland climate with warm days and cool nights across the rolling green hills.

Currency & Language

Rwandan Franc (RWF). Kinyarwanda is national, with English serving as primary for tourism.

Visa Requirements

Citizens of all countries receive a 30-day visa on arrival. US, UK, Canada visas are free.

Local Tips & Cultural Insights

  • Book Gorilla permits months in advance
  • Wear sturdy hiking boots for forest treks
  • Respect "Umuganda" monthly community work
  • Try local specialties like Brochettes and coffee
